## Demand signal

- Independent café owners post sporadically — latte art weeks, then silence. Pain matches salon pack: **blank screen between the obvious posts** (new drink photo, holiday hours).
- Search intent: "coffee shop instagram captions," "café social media ideas" — buying-adjacent, not pure how-to articles.
- Etsy/Gumroad: multiple $14–20 café caption + Canva bundles; **30** is the recurring count at this tier (visual-first packs often bundle captions as secondary).

---

## Owner language (Stage 1c)

- "I only post when we launch something new."
- "I don't know what to say besides we're open."
- "Our regulars would engage — I just don't have time to write."
- "I don't want to sound like Starbucks."

## Categories (mirror salon structure, café scenarios)

| # | Title | When to use |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | **If you're new here** | New follower — neighborhood identity, what kind of place this is |
| 2 | **Open this week** | Slow day, morning invite, hours, walk-in energy |
| 3 | **What goes into it** | Prep, the bar, sourcing, craft — not chain "hand-selected beans" |
| 4 | **A regular moment** | Returning face, order they always get, first-timer |
| 5 | **The regulars** | Vibe, team, personality — not a loyalty speech |
| 6 | **A win this week** | New menu item, good run, seasonal drop, milestone |
| 7 | **Start a conversation** | Poll/question — replies when there's no fresh hero shot |

## Voice anchors (Tier A — draft within earshot, do not swipe)

| Type | Anchor (from comps) |
|------|---------------------|
| **Short punchy** | Ready for the week. Next stop: coffee. (@cafegrumpy) |
| **Menu** | Banana Bread Latte… tastes just like the corner piece of banana bread right from the oven. (@cafegrumpy) |
| **Vibe / hours** | High & Dry Coffee Bar is your Brooklyn home away from home! We're open late… (@highdrynyc) |
| **Personality** | When they ask how I like my coffee… (@underpressurecoffeenyc) |
| **Drop energy** | we got a new drop!! UBE LATTE (@sextantcoffee) |

**Avoid (INTERNAL — do not paste into templates):** "crafted with care," "hand-selected beans," influencer aesthetics, franchise chain *style*. Show indie voice via comp-shaped specifics (named drinks, hours, drops) — not meta lines like "not a chain" or "corporate talk."

## Fill-ins

- `[your town]` / `[your neighborhood]`
- `[the drink or item]` / `[day]`
- Minimal — cafés have concrete nouns; prefer zero-fill when possible.
