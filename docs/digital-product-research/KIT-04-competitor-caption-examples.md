## Source quality bar (read first)

**Do not draft from SEO listicles or AI caption generators.** They trained our early passes to sound manufactured.

| Tier | Source type | Use for drafting? |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| **A — Primary** | Verbatim captions from **real salon/stylist Instagram posts** (independent, not franchise) | ✅ Yes — gold standard |
| **B — Secondary** | Paid pack **listing previews** (Etsy mockups), stylist-tool blogs with **accessible** template lists | ⚠️ Post-type reference only — often hashtag-heavy or templated |
| **C — Context** | Reddit/owner forums, Stage 1 owner language | Owner pain/fill-ins — not caption voice |
| **Reject** | SEO “150+ captions” blogs, AI marketing sites, dead links, caption **generators** | ❌ Never — sounds like template library / ChatGPT |

**Minimum before v3 rewrite:** **15+ Tier A lines** first. Tier B fills post-type gaps only.
