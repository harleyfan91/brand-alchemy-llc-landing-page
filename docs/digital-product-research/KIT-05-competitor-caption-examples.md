## Source quality bar (read first)

**Do not draft from SEO listicles or AI caption generators.** They trained our early passes to sound manufactured.

| Tier | Source type | Use for drafting? |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| **A — Primary** | Verbatim captions from **real café/coffee shop Instagram posts** (independent, not franchise) | ✅ Yes — gold standard |
| **B — Secondary** | Paid pack **listing previews** (Etsy mockups), café-tool blogs with **accessible** template lists | ⚠️ Post-type reference only — often hashtag-heavy or templated |
| **C — Context** | Reddit/owner forums, Stage 1 owner language | Owner pain/fill-ins — not caption voice |
| **Reject** | SEO “150+ captions” blogs, AI marketing sites (Socialmon), dead links, caption **generators** | ❌ Never — sounds like template library / ChatGPT |

**Minimum before v3 rewrite:** **15+ Tier A lines** first. Tier B fills post-type gaps only.

## How examples get into this file

| Method | What it works for |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Browser profile read** | Public Instagram profiles (no login) — post captions appear in page headings/meta; best for Tier A |
| **Instagram web_profile_info API + og:description** | Public post shortcodes + caption text without login |
| **Manual copy** | TikTok video captions, Facebook/Threads posts, Etsy listings (often login-walled or **403**) |
| **Purchase** | Digital pack PDFs — no free public samples on sales pages |

**Platform access (June 2026):**

| Platform | Automated collection | Notes |
|----------|---------------------|-------|
| **Instagram** | ✅ Works for many public profiles | Captions in `og:description` on individual posts; profile grid may redirect to login in browser |
| **TikTok** | ⚠️ Profile only | Bio + video grid load; **video captions** need opening each post |
| **Facebook** | ❌ Login wall | Business page posts not readable without account |
| **Threads** | ❌ Login wall | Same Meta auth as FB |

**Honest inventory:** Tier A below is **real account copy** sourced directly from Instagram posts — not SEO blogs. Collab/repost captions excluded when author handle ≠ café account.

**Accounts sampled (independent, not chain):** @cafegrumpy, @saintfrankcoffee, @highdrynyc, @underpressurecoffeenyc, @sextantcoffee, @heartworkcoffee — collected **June 2026** via public Instagram post meta.

## Rejected sources (do not use)

| Source | Why rejected |
|--------|----------------|
| Viral Marketing Lab / Socialmon / SocialRails café caption listicles | SEO / AI marketing; generic fill-in lines; not real café voice |
| `@devocioncoffee` (Ukraine location) | Wrong brand vs Brooklyn Devoción; non-English primary voice for US pack |
| Collab posts where author ≠ café handle | e.g. partner/event account caption on café grid — not owner voice |
