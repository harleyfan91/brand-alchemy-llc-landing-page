## Identity Kit

### Identity Kit Core
- Price: $79
- Includes:
  - Brand Brief (1-page PDF)
    - Brand anchor sentence (north-star positioning line)
    - Brand overview
    - Ideal customer
    - Core transformation / promise
    - Values and positioning cues
    - Brand story angle
    - Differentiation snapshot
  - Brand Style Guide (2-page PDF, visual-first)
    - Palette overview
    - Visual direction summary
    - Typography direction
    - Style principles
    - Do / avoid guidance
    - Practical usage notes (where to apply first)
  - Voice and Content Playbook (2-3 page PDF, writing guidance)
    - Tone profile
    - Voice guardrails
    - Messaging themes
    - Sample phrases and language cues
    - Writing do / avoid guidance
  - 30-Day Quick Start Checklist (1-page PDF rollout plan)
    - Week 1: foundational actions
    - Week 2: messaging updates
    - Week 3: visual rollout
    - Week 4: consistency checks

### Identity Kit Pro
- Price: $149
- Includes:
  - Everything in Identity Kit Core, with deeper synthesis/personalization across audience, tone, story, and differentiation
  - Content Starter Pack (practical copy asset), including:
    - Brand summary options (three one-liner angles + elevator pitch + paragraph)
    - Homepage messaging directions
    - Social bios (short profile + long About)
    - Caption starters
    - Content pillar prompts
    - CTA variations per channel (shared with Voice Playbook)
  - Pro-only **email voice templates** on Voice Playbook page 3 (not inside the Content Starter Pack PDF)
  - Practical difference vs Core: Core is strategy + direction + implementation checklist; Pro adds reusable, ready-to-adapt messaging and copy assets

## Content packs

Standalone copy (not Identity Kit deliverables, not local launch kits). All $19 content packs are **industry-specific** — each product type ships as a separate SKU per industry. The Local Kits and Identity Kit are the industry-agnostic layer; content packs are the specific layer on top.

**Production strategy:** Start with three industries (Salons & Beauty, Cafés & Coffee Shops, Home Services), prove the format, then expand. See `DIGITAL_PRODUCT_CONTENT_SCHEMA.md` for the full production backlog and matrix.

### Social Content Pack (industry-specific, $19 each)

35 ready-to-post captions organized by situation (7 categories × 5 for salon/beauty; 30–35 range for other industries per Stage 1 benchmark). Written specifically for one industry so captions use real vocabulary and real scenarios — not generic placeholders. Fill-ins are minimal ([your town], [your trade], and one specific-detail fill in customer-moment templates).

**Shipping first:**
- **Social Content Pack: Salons & Beauty** — $19
- **Social Content Pack: Cafés & Coffee Shops** — $19
- **Social Content Pack: Home Services** — $19

More industries to follow using the same format.

---

### Email Blast Pack (industry-specific, $19 each)

15–20 complete ready-to-send emails for specific moments (new offering, slow week, re-engagement, seasonal push, quick update, thank you). Each includes a subject line, 3–5 sentence body, and a closing CTA line. Written to sound like the owner typed it personally — not a newsletter template. Industry-specific versions in planning; generic framework complete.

---

### Promo & Offer Pack (industry-specific, $19 each)

25–30 captions for "ask" moments — availability, new offerings, weekend pushes, time-bounded invites, what we do explanations. Warm and direct without pressure language. Natural pair with the Social Content Pack (presence + ask). Industry-specific versions in planning; generic framework complete.

---

### Holiday & Events Pack (industry-specific, $19 each)

Ready-to-use copy for holidays and local events. Planned; format and template count TBD.

---

### Core Content Pack (industry-specific, $29 each)

Broader multi-channel templates for routine posts and updates across website, email, and directories. Planned.
